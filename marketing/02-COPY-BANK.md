# 02 — KEV Copy Bank

Every string the campaign renders, in one place. Plain text only — nothing is
baked into art — so this file is the single place to change words before
re-rendering. Facts come from `01-PRODUCT-FACT-SHEET.md`; the guardrails in §6
are hard rules.

Brand voice: warm, child-safe, school-credible. Declarative short sentences.
Numerals where they help ("11 floors · 2,234 structures · 185+ subjects").
Second person for the student, first person plural for the school. No hype, no
trading language, no performance claims.

  match. The textbook is one click from the bench.

  your movement on-device — nothing leaves your device.

  notes and chat queued for when you're back online.
