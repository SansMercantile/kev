# 03 — KEV Campaign Plan

The launch plan: positioning, audience, message pillars, channel × format
matrix, and a 6-week cadence. Follows the Zeely playbook adapted for a
child-safe education product (no trading language, no performance claims).

---

## Positioning

For parents and schools who want learning that holds attention, KEV is the
all-in-one immersive school — a walkable 3D campus, real science labs, AI
tutors for every subject, a virtual library and sports — because children learn
best when they can walk to class, take things apart, and play.

Three owned words, in order: **Immersive** (you're inside it, not watching it),
**Real** (real labs, real tutors, real offline — not marketing), **Yours**
(your school, your level, your pace, your data stays yours).

---

## Audience

| Segment | Who | Lead message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parents (K-12) | Want screen time that teaches | "One school, every way to learn — and it works offline." |
| Schools & teachers | Need engagement + cover for labs/PE they can't run | "Real labs and PE you couldn't otherwise offer, role-gated." |
| Students (13+) | Bored by recorded video | "Dissect the atlas. Steer 4D. Play the sport. Walk the campus." |
| Homeschool co-ops | Need a full school in a box | "Campus, tutors, library, sports — all-in-one, offline-capable." |

**Anti-persona:** not for buyers who want recorded-video courses, a game with
a lesson bolted on, or data-harvesting "free" apps. KEV is honest immersive
education, not surveillance-adjacent engagement.

---

## Message pillars (each maps to a landing page + content batch)

1. **The Campus** — walk to class through a real 3D school (11 floors, 199 rooms).
2. **The Labs** — studio-grade 3D: atlas, vehicle, chemistry, gravity, cell, optics.
3. **The Tutors** — AI tutors for every subject, level-adaptive, Socratic.
4. **Offline & Access** — works with no internet, on any device, role-gated, 6 languages.
5. **Sports & Motion** — camera PE + smartwatch pulse, on-device and private.
6. **Frontier Ideas** — Quantum Lab (real Ising solver), Dimension Explorer (real 4D).

## 6-week cadence

- **Wk 1** — Launch: brand page live, first LinkedIn article ("One school,
  every way to learn"), poster Set A, launch blog post.
- **Wk 2** — Labs pillar: lab page, atlas article, poster Set B, dissection video.
- **Wk 3** — Tutors pillar: tutors page, Socratic article, poster Set C.
- **Wk 4** — Offline pillar: offline page, "no wifi" article, poster Set D.
- **Wk 5** — Motion pillar: Motion Gym page, camera-PE article, dance video, poster Set E.
- **Wk 6** — Frontier pillar: frontier page (Quantum + Dimensions), Ising +
  tesseract articles, poster Set F. Review flywheel starts (early families).

---

## KPIs (no vanity metrics)

- Landing-page unique visits per pillar (programmatic-SEO read).
- Owned conversion: "Start free" click-through to signup.
- LinkedIn: article completions + profile visits (ClickUp-tracked drafts).
- Review flywheel: count of early-family reviews collected (target: real, not incentivized).
- App: new signups → first lab opened (activation), offline sessions on low-connectivity devices.

---

## Guardrails (carry into every asset)

- All facts from `01-PRODUCT-FACT-SHEET.md` — if it's not on screen, it's not in an ad.
- No performance/learning-outcome claims. No literal "5D". No quantum-hardware
  claims. No camera-surveillance framing. No fake multiplayer.
- Child-safe imagery and language throughout; the house gold, never alarm-red.
