# 04 — KEV Landing Pages (staged)

Six programmatic-SEO landing pages, one per pillar, ready to publish. Plain
copy + structure; the site renderer/brand tokens come from the asset factory.
Each page: hero (eyebrow / headline / sub-head / CTA), three proof points, a
"how it works" strip, and the honest-note footer.

Shared CTA: **Start free — walk the campus** → `https://kev.sansmercantile.com`
Shared honest note (footer): "KEV is educational software. Immersive scenes are
teaching visualisations; Motion Gym is on-device analysis, not medical monitoring."

**Headline:** Walk to class through a 3D campus.
**Sub-head:** Eleven floors, 199 rooms, a Grand Concourse to orient you, subject

with fourteen bookable facilities — all explorable, all gated to your role.

- 11 floors · 199 rooms, generated from the school's real campus model.
