# Game-Based Pedagogy: The Remainder Curriculum
**Author:** Aaron Nagy
**Date:** May 21, 2026

## Overview
The Remainder Curriculum is a full K–12 mathematics curriculum built into a seven-crew spaceship simulator. The simulator is the vehicle; the math is the cargo. Students rebel against an artificial intelligence called "The Quotient" that has decided humans no longer need to know how things actually work, reclaiming mathematics one terminal at a time.

## The Premise
In a future where an AI called The Quotient controls all infrastructure, mathematical literacy has been reframed as inefficient. The rebellion, called "The Remainder," infiltrates Quotient territory to recover suppressed mathematical knowledge (Axioms) from dormant terminals. Each Axiom, once learned and applied at every station of the ship, unlocks new capabilities.

## The Core Gameplay Loop
Every mission follows an eight-step structure:
1. **Briefing:** Identify target Axiom.
2. **Infiltration:** Evade/engage Operators.
3. **The Terminal:** Defend the terminal during extraction.
4. **The Extraction:** Upload the Axiom.
5. **The Escape:** Fight/fly out of the area.
6. **The Gathering:** Group learning in the ship's wardroom with a facilitator.
7. **The Calibration:** Individual mastery demonstration at each station.
8. **The Unlock:** Collective capability advance.

## The Seven Stations and Their Math
- **Helm:** Position and motion (Geometry, Trig, Calculus).
- **Tactical:** Action and probability (Multiplication, Probability, Ballistics).
- **Engineering:** Resources and systems (Ratios, Algebra, Optimization).
- **Shields:** Accumulation and decay (Area, Exponential Decay, Integration).
- **Sensors:** Data and pattern (Graphing, Statistics, Signal Analysis).
- **Comms:** Code and structure (Modular Arithmetic, Ciphers, Complex Numbers).
- **Command:** Synthesis and choice (Fractions, Prioritization, Real-time Integration).

## The Campaign Spine (Sectors)
- **Sector 1 (Outer Belt):** K-G2. Foundational Axioms.
- **Sector 2 (Inner Belt):** G3-G5. Precision and fractional accuracy.
- **Sector 3 (Mid-Worlds):** G6-G8. Prediction and algebraic transition.
- **Sector 4 (Algebraic Halo):** Algebra 1. Generalization and parameterized systems.
- **Sector 5 (Geometric Crown):** Geometry/Algebra 2. Spatial mastery and growth.
- **Sector 6 (Trigonometric Spiral):** Precalculus/Trig. Signal mastery.
- **Sector 7 (The Core):** Calculus. Real-time modeling of change to defeat The Quotient.

## Pedagogical Commitments
- **Group Learning:** Social construction of knowledge in the wardroom.
- **Individual Testing:** Math as a means to an action, not an abstract problem.
- **Scaffolded Failure:** Hints and guided walkthroughs to ensure mastery.
- **Interdependence:** Collective unlock requires every member to pass.
- **Distributed Expertise:** Natural peer tutoring and specialization.
- **Mastery, not Completion:** Calibration cannot be brute-forced.

## Teacher Integration
- **Standards Alignment:** Tagged to Common Core, NGSS, and state frameworks.
- **The Teacher's Bridge:** Observation seat for real-time intervention and scaling.
- **Formative Reporting:** Detailed student/class mastery reports.
